How to Play Jackaroo

🎯 Objective

Your goal is to move all 4 of your marbles from your Home Zone to your Home Base by navigating the board using card draws and strategic plays.

🃏 The Deck

  • 102 cards total with special action cards.
  • Aces and Kings allow marbles to enter the board.
  • Jokers and custom cards add twists to gameplay.

🔄 Turn Structure

  • Draw a card at the beginning of your turn.
  • Use it to move a marble, attack, or block an opponent.
  • If you can't play a card, discard it.

🏁 Board Zones

  • Home Zone – where your marbles start.
  • Safe Zone – marbles here can't be captured.
  • Trap Cells – destroy marbles that land on them.
  • Fire Pits – dangerous areas with random effects.
Tip: Use Safe Zones wisely to protect your lead. Avoid Trap Cells and plan your moves based on what cards remain.
