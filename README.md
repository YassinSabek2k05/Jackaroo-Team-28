# 🏆 Jackaroo: A New Game Spin

## 🎲 Introduction
Jackaroo is a strategic board and card game with deep roots in Middle Eastern culture, especially in the Gulf region. Traditionally played in teams, this project reimagines Jackaroo as a **single-player experience**, where one player competes against three AI-controlled opponents.

## 🔄 What's New?
This adaptation introduces several exciting changes to enhance gameplay:
- 🎮 **Single-player mode**: Compete against 3 AI players.
- 🃏 **Revamped card mechanics**: A deck of 102 cards with unique actions.
- 🎯 **New board elements**: Home Zones, Safe Zones, Trap Cells, and Fire Pits.
- 🚀 **Improved movement rules**:
  - Marbles enter the track only with an Ace or King.
  - Trap Cells change position after each marble is destroyed.
  - Safe Zones offer complete protection.
- 🔁 **Turn-based play**: Each player takes turns clockwise, playing one card per turn.
- 🤖 **Smart AI**: CPU players randomly select and execute actions.

## 🛠️ Game Setup
- **Players**: 1 human vs. 3 AI players.
- **Board**: A 100-cell track-based board.
- **Marbles**: Each player starts with 4 marbles in their Home Zone.
- **Deck**: 102 custom-designed cards.
- **Special Zones**:
  - 🏠 **Home Zone**: Starting point for marbles.
  - 🔄 **Base Cell**: Entry point onto the track.
  - 🏁 **Safe Zone**: Final destination, immune from attacks.
  - ⚠️ **Trap Cells**: Destroy marbles, sending them back to Home.
  - 🔥 **Fire Pit**: Discarded cards pile.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Game Start**: Enter your name and get assigned a color. AI players get unique colors.
2. **Turn Structure**:
   - Players take turns clockwise.
   - Each player starts with 4 cards per round.
   - Play a card to move, attack, or defend.
   - Invalid moves result in automatic card discarding.
3. **Marble Movement**:
   - Moves depend on card ranks.
   - Marbles cannot pass through their own marbles.
   - Special cards allow unique interactions like burning or saving marbles.
4. **Winning Condition**: The first player to move all 4 marbles into the Safe Zone wins!
